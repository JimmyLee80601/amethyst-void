Straight answers

Before you ask

Is Harley Angel a real person?

No. Harley Angel is a virtual model — a fictional persona rendered with AI image tools. Her face, hair, and foot tattoos are a fixed character design, kept consistent across every shoot. Every Amethyst Void post carries an AI-generated label, and this page says it plainly: you're collecting artwork of a character, not photos of a person.

How do custom shoots work?

Build your brief above and download it. When you order, the studio shoots to that brief — your theme, polish, props, and must-haves — with Harley's signature ink and toe shape locked in. Text tattoos are the hardest detail in AI imagery, so customs include rerolls until the "Mr. Easton" script reads properly. Simple customs are often turned around the same day.

What do I actually receive?

Full-resolution image files (or video clips, for video customs) delivered digitally. Sets are yours to keep and enjoy privately. Commercial reuse — printing for resale, using Harley to advertise a product — needs a separate conversation first, because she's the brand's identity, not stock art.

Where do you post?

Instagram, TikTok, and Pinterest are the free storefronts — daily frames, polish drops, and behind-the-scenes of each shoot being built. Paid sets and customs are sold direct from the studio rather than through foot-content marketplaces; this page is home base for both.

Can I request something you haven't shot yet?

That's the best kind of request. New themes, odd props, seasonal ideas — if it keeps Harley recognizable and stays within the brand's tasteful-editorial lane, the studio will take a run at it. Build the brief and ask.
